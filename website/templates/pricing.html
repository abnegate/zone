How buying works

Zone never handles your money. It verifies a key on your own server.

  1. Check out

    Buy Zone Team or Zone Enterprise with Polar, monthly or yearly, for the number of seats you need.

  2. Get your key

    Your license key is emailed to the billing contact on the order.

  3. Paste it in

    An admin pastes the key into Settings → License. Your plan unlocks in place: same image, same data, no reinstall.

What counts as a seat

A seat is an active member: anyone who signed in during the calendar month. Members who are deactivated, or who did not sign in that month, are free.

Community counts members the same way and allows up to {{ communityMemberLimit }} active members per server.

For example: an organization has 40 members, and 32 of them sign in during March. That is 32 seats for March.

On Team and Enterprise, seats are soft-enforced. If you go over your licensed count, admins see a banner and the difference is trued up at renewal. Nobody is ever blocked from signing in because of a seat count.

Licensing questions

Does Zone contact a license server?

Only to renew. A license key is signed, and Zone checks the signature on your server against a public key built into Zone, with no call out. Once a day, in the two weeks before a subscription key expires, a server that can reach the internet sends its current key, and nothing else, to fetch the renewed one. Set ZONE_LICENSE_RENEWAL=false to turn that off and paste keys yourself.

Do Team and Enterprise work in an air-gapped install?

Yes. Because keys are verified offline, an install with no internet access verifies a key exactly like any other. Paste the key into Settings → License as usual. A packaged air-gapped install bundle is coming.

What happens when our license expires?

You get a 30-day grace period after the expiry date. After that, expiry still never locks anyone out or loosens security: SSO login keeps working and existing policies keep being enforced. What pauses until you renew is editing policies, and audit export and streaming.

What if we use more seats than we bought?

On Team and Enterprise, nothing is blocked. Admins see a banner showing the overage, and the extra seats are trued up at renewal at your plan’s per-seat price.

What happens at the Community member limit?

Once {{ communityMemberLimit }} members have signed in during a calendar month, nobody new can join the server. Reaching the limit never signs anyone out. A Team or Enterprise key removes the limit on the same install.

Can we change our seat count or renew?

Yes. Renewals and seat changes go through Polar, and each one emails a new key to your billing contact. A server that can reach the internet installs it by itself, unless automatic renewal is turned off; otherwise paste it into Settings → License to replace the old one. If you cancel, your current key keeps working until it expires.

Can we move from Team to Enterprise?

Yes. Change your plan in Polar, and a new key is emailed to your billing contact. A server that can reach the internet picks it up when it next renews. To switch sooner, or if automatic renewal is turned off, paste it into Settings → License.

Who handles sales tax and VAT?

Polar is the merchant of record. It calculates, collects and remits VAT, GST and US sales tax where they apply, and shows any tax at checkout. Prices on this page are in {{ currency }}.

Do we get an invoice?

Polar handles payment, receipts and invoices. Larger deals can be invoiced through Polar at the same per-seat price; email us.

Do we need to reinstall to upgrade from Community?

No. Community, Team and Enterprise are the same image. Without a license Zone runs as Community; pasting a license unlocks your plan on your existing install and data.

Can our security team read the paid code?

Yes. The code behind Team and Enterprise, including the license check, is in the public repository under the Zone Enterprise License. See Licensing.

Is anything in Community capped or time-limited?

Community allows up to {{ communityMemberLimit }} active members per server. Nothing else is capped, and it never expires.
