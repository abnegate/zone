  3. Paste it in

    An admin pastes the key into Settings → License. Enterprise features unlock in place: same image, same data, no reinstall.

What counts as a seat

A seat is an organization member who signed in during the calendar month. Members who are deactivated, or who did not sign in that month, are free.

For example: an organization has 40 members, and 32 of them sign in during March. That is 32 seats for March.

Seats are soft-enforced. If you go over your licensed count, admins see a banner and the difference is trued up at renewal. Nobody is ever blocked from signing in because of a seat count.

Licensing questions

Does Zone contact a license server?

Only to renew. A license key is signed, and Zone checks the signature on your server against a public key built into Zone, with no call out. Once a day, in the two weeks before a subscription key expires, a server that can reach the internet sends its current key, and nothing else, to fetch the renewed one. Set ZONE_LICENSE_RENEWAL=false to turn that off and paste keys yourself.

Does Zone Enterprise work in an air-gapped install?

Yes. Because keys are verified offline, an install with no internet access verifies a key exactly like any other. Paste the key into Settings → License as usual. A packaged air-gapped install bundle is coming.

What happens when our license expires?

You get a 30-day grace period after the expiry date. After that, expiry still never locks anyone out or loosens security: SSO login keeps working and existing policies keep being enforced. What pauses until you renew is editing policies, and audit export and streaming.

What if we use more seats than we bought?

Nothing is blocked. Admins see a banner showing the overage, and the extra seats are trued up at renewal at your per-seat price.

Can we change our seat count or renew?

Yes. Renewals and seat changes go through Polar, and each one emails a new key to your billing contact. A server that can reach the internet installs it by itself, unless automatic renewal is turned off; otherwise paste it into Settings → License to replace the old one. If you cancel, your current key keeps working until it expires.

Do we need to reinstall to upgrade from Community?
