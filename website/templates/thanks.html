Activate Zone Enterprise

  1. Find the key

    Open the email with your license key and copy the whole key. It is one long string.

  2. Open Settings → License

    Sign in to your Zone console as an organization admin and go to Settings → License.

  3. Paste and save

    Paste the key and save it. Zone verifies it on your own server and unlocks the Enterprise features in place. There is nothing to reinstall.

Keep the email. When you renew or change your seat count, a new key is emailed to the same contact. A server that can reach the internet installs it by itself, unless automatic renewal is turned off; otherwise paste it in the same place.

Key not arrived? Check your spam folder first, then email {{ contactEmail }} with the email address on your order.
